Service Description

The ALPLM Security Alert System delivers automated SMS notifications to designated security personnel when a presence sensor detects activity in a monitored area. This is an internal operational tool, not a consumer-facing service.

Eligibility

SMS alerts are delivered only to individuals who have been explicitly designated as authorized recipients by the system operator. There is no public opt-in.

Message Content

All messages are automated security notifications. No marketing, promotional, or unsolicited messages will be sent.

Opting Out

Recipients may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for support contact information.

Limitations

The system operator makes no guarantee of delivery timing or availability. SMS delivery is subject to carrier conditions.
