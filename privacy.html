1. Overview

Ninjaneering Specialties operates the ALPLM Security Alert System, an automated IoT presence-detection system that sends SMS notifications to a small number of pre-authorized security personnel. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect personal information in connection with our SMS alerting service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information solely for the purpose of delivering SMS security alerts:

  • Full name of the registered recipient
  • Mobile phone number of the registered recipient
  • Date and time of opt-in submission

No additional personal data is collected. We do not collect location data, device identifiers, or any information through cookies or tracking technologies.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your mobile phone number is used exclusively to deliver automated SMS security alert messages from Ninjaneering Specialties. We will not use your number for marketing, promotional, or any other purpose.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information with third parties for their own use. Your information may be transmitted to our SMS delivery provider (Twilio, Inc.) solely for the purpose of message delivery. Twilio's privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy at twilio.com/legal/privacy.

5. Data Retention

Opt-in records are retained for as long as you remain an active recipient or as required to demonstrate regulatory compliance. You may request deletion at any time by contacting nickninjaneer@gmail.com.

6. SMS Opt-Out

You may opt out of receiving SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message received from Ninjaneering Specialties, or by contacting nickninjaneer@gmail.com. Upon opt-out, your number will be removed from the active recipient list promptly.

7. TCPA Compliance

Ninjaneering Specialties complies with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA). SMS messages are sent only to individuals who have provided express written consent via the opt-in form at https://nickninjaneer.github.io/CaseSensor/. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency: fewer than 10 messages per month.

8. Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect collected information. Recipient phone numbers are stored only in secure form submission records and are not published or made accessible to the public.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
