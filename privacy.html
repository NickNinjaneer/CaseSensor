Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

Overview

The ALPLM Security Alert System is an automated IoT sensor that sends SMS notifications to a small number of pre-authorized security personnel when presence is detected in a monitored area.

Information We Collect

We collect only the mobile phone number(s) of authorized recipients, provided directly by the system operator. No personal data is collected from recipients, and no data is collected via web forms or public sign-ups.

How We Use Information

Phone numbers are used solely to deliver automated security alert messages. They are not shared with third parties, sold, or used for any marketing purpose.

Message Frequency

Messages are event-driven and low volume (estimated fewer than 10 per month). Recipients may reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe.
